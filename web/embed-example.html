How to add it to a page

<div id="ar-crash-map" data-height="70vh"></div>
<script src="https://maps.arkansasonline.com/crashes/embed.js"></script>

Optional attributes on the container:

<div id="ar-crash-map"
     data-height="460px"          <!-- height of the MAP; controls and legend
                                       sit outside it, so the block is taller -->
     data-center="-94.16,36.06"   <!-- lng,lat -->
     data-zoom="10"
     data-measure="ped_count"     <!-- crash_count | fatalities | injuries |
                                       ped_count | cmv_count | impaired_count -->
     data-year="2024"
     data-title="Pedestrian crashes in Fayetteville"
     data-hint="Zoom in for details on individual crashes"
     data-source="Source: Arkansas State Patrol via ARDOT"></div>

Pass data-title="", data-hint="" or data-source="" to drop any of those lines — useful when the article headline or a standing credit already says it.

For more than one map on a page, give each container the ar-crash-map class instead of the id. To place a map yourself (inside a tab or a lazily-rendered module), skip the auto-init and call ARCrashMap.init(element) once the container is in the DOM.
